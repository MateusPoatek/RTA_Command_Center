RTA Board Engineering Guide

For engineers new to the project. It explains what the board does, where each number comes from, and what we learned from real floor data.

At a glance

  • The RTA Board shows only exceptions. It joins Verint (schedule and activity), GTT (clock-in and clock-out) and, later, Workday.
  • Six alert rules with three severities replace the ANA Bot's single, unranked alert. Only Sev 1 goes straight to the Team Lead.
  • Every KPI is a ratio of sums over the elapsed published schedule, measured as of the Verint capture time, not the wall clock.
  • Verint has no API access yet. A Chrome extension captures data in the RTA's browser and pushes it to the board every 30 seconds.
  • Most bugs so far were data-shape problems, not code problems. Section 8 lists eight, each with its measured impact.

1. Why this exists

The project started from a floor visit to the El Salvador and Guatemala sites. The Real-Time Analysts (RTAs) and Team Leads (TLs) described the same problems.

  • Chat overload. The alert group chats carry "100 messages nobody reads".
  • TLs ignore group chats. An alert for the whole floor belongs to nobody.
  • False alerts erode trust. Approved absences and short breaks show up as violations, so people stop reading all alerts.

The baseline is the ANA Bot. It sends one alert type every 15 minutes. The type is "GTT activity differs from the Verint schedule". It has no severity, no acknowledgement and no escalation. The board replaces that with rules, severities, one card per agent, a TL screen with an "On it" button, and an RTA floor view.

The board has three screens.

ScreenAudienceQuestion it answers
My teamTeam LeadWhich of my people need me now? One sentence and one button per row.
FloorRTA (also wall mode)Where is it burning, and which TL has not acted? One row per TL, worst first.
DetailsWFM and engineersHow the numbers are made: KPI tiles, shrinkage funnel, data checks, data sources.

3. Data captured

All examples below are synthetic. The field catalogue lives in RTA_Board_V2/src/data/lineage.ts. The Details screen and gtt-verint-data-lineage.html render the same data.

3.1 Verint structure

The extension reads the calendar-filter response. It holds one resourceData[] entry per employee, with publishedSchedule, draftSchedule and actualSchedule events.

eventTypeMeaningPrecedence
ShiftAssignmentContainer for the whole shift. Counts as work.1
ShiftEventAssignmentActivity inside the shift. isPaid true is a paid break. isPaid false is unpaid lunch.5 / 10
CalendarEventMeetings placed on top of the shift.20
TimeOffEventPlanned absence.70

parse.js resolves the events by precedence. At each instant the highest precedence wins. The result is a list of segments of kind work, paid, unpaid or off. The extension sends them as published_segments_json.

actualSchedule events give the actual shift_start and shift_end. Verint adds an activity only when it closes, so shift_end is the end of the last closed activity. adherenceSummary is one interval for the whole day, so it is not "as of now". workResourceDetails.organizationName is the real line of business. It follows the pattern BPO_<Client>_<Site>_<LOB>.

3.6 The join

Verint writes "Last, First". GTT writes "First Middle Last Last2". The board joins in this order.

  1. Workday ID when the Verint row carries one. It does not yet, so this path is dormant.
  2. Exact name-token match. Both names are split into words and sorted, then compared.
  3. Loose match. Accents are ignored. The two names must share at least 2 words. The pair must also be each other's unique best. A tie on either side never matches.

Exact matching alone linked 20 of 87 clock-ins. See lesson 7 for the loose rule's validation.

4. Pipeline

This is one full cycle, from the RTA's click to the numbers on screen.

sequenceDiagram
  participant RTA as RTA in browser
  participant INJ as inject.js (page world)
  participant CV as content_verint.js
  participant BG as background.js
  participant CC as content_cc.js
  participant BD as Board (tab on :5174)
  participant API as local_api.py
  RTA->>BD: Pick account (set-lob)
  BD->>CC: postMessage set-lob
  CC->>BG: set LOB
  RTA->>INJ: Full Capture
  INJ->>INJ: Census pages (useAllEmployees = true)
  INJ->>INJ: Targeted chunks of 50 ids, 800 ms apart
  INJ->>CV: Calendar-filter responses
  CV->>BG: Relay captures
  BG->>BG: rebuild() with parse.js extractRoster, filter by ACCOUNT_ORGS
  loop every 30 s
    BD->>CC: push-now
    CC->>BG: push-now
    BG->>CC: roster
    CC->>BD: postMessage kind cards, roster, syncedAt
  end
  loop every 5 min
    BD->>API: POST /gtt-events
    API-->>BD: clock_in / clock_out events
  end
  BD->>BD: joinAll, buildBoard (alerts), computeKpis (as of capture)
  1. Tee. inject.js runs in the page world. It copies Verint's calendar-filter responses as the page receives them.
  2. Full Capture. runFullCapture pages through a census with useAllEmployees = true until a page returns fewer than 50 rows. It then pulls schedule and adherence detail for every indexed id in chunks of 50, with 800 ms between calls, one at a time.
  3. Rebuild. background.js keeps captures per LOB and calls extractRoster from parse.js. It keeps only rows whose Verint organization matches the account in ACCOUNT_ORGS.
  4. Push. The extension posts {__rtaLiveBridge:true, kind:"cards", roster, syncedAt} to the board tab through content_cc.js. The board sends set-lob, trigger-capture and push-now back the same way.
  5. Compute. The board re-runs the alert rules on a 15-second clock, so "for N min" keeps counting between captures. The KPIs use the capture time as their "now" and only move when a new capture arrives (lesson 4).

5. Alert rules

The rules live in RTA_Board_V2/src/domain/exceptions.ts. Every threshold is one field in DEFAULT. An account can override fields in ACCOUNTS. The full rationale is in RTA_Board_V2/docs/ALERT_RULES.md.

Modifiers and routing

  • Repetition. The 3rd out-of-adherence episode of the day (each at least 5 minutes) bumps A2 or A3 up one level.
  • One card per agent. If several rules fire, the worst severity wins and the others are listed on the card.
  • Suppression. Trainees, Workday-excused agents and RTA-declared outages are counted but get no row.
  • Delivery. Sev 1 goes directly to the TL, automatically. Sev 2 appears in the TL digest. Sev 3 stays on the board.
  • Escalation. It is opt-in per account, and EPIC has it on. A Sev 1 not acknowledged after 5 minutes goes to the Ops Manager. Still unacknowledged 10 minutes later (15 in total), it goes to the WFM Director.
  • Acknowledgement. The TL presses "On it". A reason is optional. The list comes from the ANA SOP: VPN issue, GTT glitch, Windows glitch, power or internet outage, hardware, long chat or call, approved but not in the system, other. A reason explains an alert. It never reclassifies it for the scorecard.

"Resolved" means the rule stops firing on a later capture. The board has no manual close. Acknowledgement and send times are held in memory in this version, so a page reload clears them.

6. KPIs and formulas

The code is RTA_Board_V2/src/domain/kpis.ts. Every figure is a ratio of sums over the elapsed part of today's published schedule. This follows the scorecard SOP. It is never an average of per-agent percentages.

Ground rules

  • Trainees are excluded.
  • Unpublished schedules are excluded, because there is no base to measure against. They are counted and shown.
  • Base is the paid published minutes so far (work plus paid segments) plus planned time off so far. It is clipped to the start of Verint's capture window, estimated as the earliest Verint shift start in the roster.
  • As-of time per agent. It is the agent's last closed Verint activity when that is 30 minutes old or less. Otherwise it is the capture time. An agent silent for more than 30 minutes counts as shrink.
  • "Now" is the capture time, not the wall clock.

Formulas

Attendance  = present / (present + unexplained absent)
              approved absences (Workday exception, or Verint time off with no paid time) are left out

Adherence   = in-adherence min / (in + out)
              out = min(paid so far, Verint out-of-adherence minutes today)
              in  = paid so far - out

Productive hours = sum over present agents of min(paid elapsed, productive minutes) / 60
              productive minutes = planned/actual label match, else Verint productive paid minutes

Shrinkage   = (absence minutes + in-shift non-productive minutes) / base
              absence minutes   = elapsed time of agents who never started, plus time off of those who did
              in-shift non-prod = paid elapsed - productive minutes, for present agents
              base              = sum of (paid elapsed + planned time off elapsed) over due agents

Most common violation = the most frequent label among the active exceptions on the board

A "due" agent has a published schedule and either a started shift (Verint activity or GTT clock-in) or a scheduled start that has passed. Present means the agent has a Verint activity or a GTT clock-in. If GTT shows a clock-in but Verint shows no activity, the agent is present with zero productive minutes. The funnel counts that case separately as "GTT only".

The shrinkage funnel

The funnel records each step so any shrinkage figure can be traced. The counts below are from a real capture of EPIC El Salvador on 2026-10-09 at 13:18 local time. Counts only, no names.

7. Scorecard and finance

Which scorecard metrics each KPI feeds

The WFM Weekly Scorecard has about 35 metrics. Everything descends from base scheduled minutes, which is Verint's published schedule. The mapping below follows POC_Redefined/Product_and_Architecture_docs/SCORECARD_DATA_PRIORITIES.md and lineage.ts.

What the numbers mean for finance

  • Headcount. Shrinkage sets required headcount: FTE = workload hours / (1 - shrinkage). A higher shrinkage needs more people for the same work.
  • Paid time with no output. Paid minutes that are not productive are cost with no billable output.
  • Contract exposure. Unplanned absence and adherence drive SLA and contract risk. One account has an unplanned-absence target of "below 9%".
  • Pay and service. Adherence also drives agent bonuses and interval service level.
ILLUSTRATIVE example, not a forecast. Take 150 agents scheduled for 9 paid hours each. That is 1,350 paid hours per day. Each shrinkage point is 13.5 hours per day, about 1.5 FTE-days. At an illustrative loaded cost of $15 per hour, each point is about $200 per day. Real rates vary by geography.

8. Eight lessons from real data

All eight came from captures of a live floor. Each shows the symptom, the cause, the fix and the measured impact.

1. Unpublished schedules

Symptom
Ratios looked wrong early in the day.
Cause
90 of 264 agents had no published schedule (day off or draft). Counting them distorts attendance, productive hours and shrinkage.
Fix
Exclude them from every KPI and show the count.
Impact
The base is now 174 agents with a real schedule.

2. Unpaid lunch in the base

Symptom
Base minutes were too high.
Cause
The first-to-last event span includes unpaid lunch. It was 11.9% of the shift span on the June capture.
Fix
The extension now sends paid segments. The base uses them. The span is only a fallback, and the funnel counts it.
Impact
Unpaid lunch no longer counts as scheduled work time.

3. Verint capture window

Symptom
20 overnight agents showed 66% non-productive. Shrinkage read 49%.
Cause
Overnight shifts were published from 02:00Z, but Verint actuals begin at 07:00Z. Five hours of base had no possible activity.
Fix
Start the base at the window start, estimated as the earliest Verint shift start.
Impact
Shrinkage fell from 49% to 9.8%.

4. Wall-clock creep

Symptom
Shrinkage climbed on its own between captures.
Cause
KPIs were recomputed every 15 seconds with the wall clock. Productive minutes only change when a new capture arrives.
Fix
Compute KPIs as of the capture time.
Impact
The headline holds still between captures.

5. Verint posting lag

Symptom
Shrinkage read 14.8%.
Cause
Verint adds an activity only when it closes, in blocks of about 15 minutes. 83 of 156 agents were 15 to 17 minutes behind the capture.
Fix
Measure each agent up to the last closed activity, if it is 30 minutes old or less.
Impact
14.8% became 9.3%. Across 8 captures it stayed between 9.1% and 9.9%.

6. Whole-floor capture

Symptom
The EPIC roster held other clients' agents.
Cause
Full Capture reads every client the Verint user can see. 11,705 alerts were staged. 163 of 314 roster rows (52%) belonged to other clients.
Fix
Filter by Verint organization (ACCOUNT_ORGS).
Impact
The board sees only the account. Verint still serves the whole floor. Fewer calls and a server-side version are in the backlog.

7. Name join

Symptom
Most clock-ins did not match a Verint agent.
Cause
Verint writes "Last, First", GTT writes "First Middle Last Last2". Exact keys matched 20 of 87 GTT clock-ins.
Fix
Add the loose, unique-best match with accents ignored and at least 2 shared words.
Impact
87 of 87 matched. Of 67 loose matches checked, 66 clocked in within 15 minutes of the Verint start. The outlier matched its schedule to the second.

8. Time off and no-shows

Symptom
Agents on leave were alerted, and missed shifts stayed red for hours.
Cause
2 agents on full-day Verint time off fired "not clocked in". A finished, missed shift kept firing A1.
Fix
Suppress A1 for time off. After the shift ends, show a grey no-show: "time off not in Verint?".
Impact
No false red rows. Shrinkage stayed at 8.9% because the KPIs already classified both cases correctly.
